What Is Quick Fact Checker?

QuickFactChecker is a machine learning–based web app that helps detect whether a news article is real or fake. It uses models like Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and LSTM — trained on the LIAR dataset — to evaluate credibility and help users identify potentially misleading information.

We aim to empower individuals to make informed decisions in an era of rapid information sharing.
